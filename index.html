Featured Project

Autonomous Vehicle Challenge 2024-2026

The Autonomous vehicle challenge vehicle was a University based competition for the state of Arkansas. Our vehicle in 2024-2025 took 2nd place and in 2025-2026 took 1st place. The project features many complex systems combined to compete in environments with everchanging distractions.

Developed custom inverse kinematics for the RO-ARM m3 pro robotic arm, trained a custom YOLO v11 object detection AI model for detecting colored balls/buckets, and integrated a Monte-Carlo particle filter occupancy map based localization model for predicting the robot's pose alongside video feed and sensor visualization.
